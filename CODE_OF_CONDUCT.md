# Code of Conduct

Drift should be a useful, respectful place to build software together.

Community members are expected to communicate constructively, welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels, respect boundaries, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than individuals. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Serious conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than posted publicly.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, and other spaces represented as part of the Drift project.
